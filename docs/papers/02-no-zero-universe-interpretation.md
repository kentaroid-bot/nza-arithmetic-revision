# No-Zero Universe Interpretation

## A Speculative Research Program About Apparent Disappearance

**Abbreviation:** NZUI

**Status:** `proposal`

**Claim type:** ontological and physical interpretation

## Abstract

The No-Zero Universe Interpretation asks whether apparent local disappearance
should be interpreted as transfer, transformation, or movement beyond an
observational boundary rather than absolute nonexistence. It is motivated by the
intuition behind N-Zero Arithmetic but does not present that intuition as an
established physical law.

NZUI currently offers no novel empirical prediction. It is therefore a research
interpretation and question-generating framework, not a validated cosmology or
replacement for standard mathematics or physics.

## 1. Interpretive Thesis

The minimal thesis is:

> An observation of zero within a declared measurement boundary is evidence of
> local absence at that resolution, not sufficient evidence of absolute
> nonexistence across every boundary and representation.

This thesis does not claim that every lost object survives unchanged. Transfer,
conversion, decoherence, dissipation, information loss at a selected level, and
measurement failure are different possibilities and must not be conflated.

## 2. Three Meanings of Zero

NZUI distinguishes at least three uses:

1. **Mathematical zero:** an additive identity in a formal system.
2. **Operational zero:** a measurement reported as zero within resolution and
   calibration limits.
3. **Ontological nothingness:** a claim that no relevant entity, effect,
   relation, or residual exists anywhere in the stated domain.

The first is mathematically indispensable. The second is common and valid. The
third is a stronger metaphysical claim and cannot be inferred from the first two
without additional evidence.

## 3. Relation to NZCM

The N-Zero Conservation Model supplies vocabulary for boundaries, local states,
environment accounts, transfers, and unresolved discrepancies. It does not
prove NZUI.

NZCM can demonstrate that a modeled transfer preserves a declared quantity.
NZUI asks whether a similar interpretation is appropriate for a physical domain.
That question requires domain-specific theory and observation.

## 4. Requirements for a Physical Hypothesis

For NZUI to become testable in a particular domain, a proposal must specify:

- the physical quantity or information measure;
- the observation boundary and resolution;
- the candidate reservoir or transformation channel;
- the governing transition law;
- a prediction that differs from established models;
- an observation that could count against the proposal;
- uncertainty and error treatment.

Writing `infinity_universe` beside a local value satisfies none of these
requirements by itself.

## 5. Status of Earlier Analogies

Earlier NZA texts mentioned thermodynamics, quantum vacuum effects, general
relativity, quantum mechanics, and many-worlds interpretations. In the absence
of explicit mappings and discriminating predictions, these should be labeled
analogies or research prompts.

They must not be presented as derivations, confirmations, or applications of
NZUI. Each topic has distinct definitions of conservation, vacuum, information,
boundary, and observability.

## 6. Infinity

NZUI does not require the universe total to be represented by mathematical
infinity. An infinite constant makes every finite discrepancy invisible:

```text
infinity + q = infinity
```

That identity cannot show where `q` went. A finite domain model with explicit
reservoirs is more informative whenever measurements permit it.

Whether the physical universe is finite or infinite remains outside the current
claim. A cyclic universe, an infinite universe, and a finite conserved system
are separate hypotheses.

## 7. Negative Values

Negative values may represent direction, orientation, charge convention,
deficit, or a coordinate relative to a reference. NZUI does not reject them.

The interpretive question is instead whether a negative label has been mistaken
for negative existence. Domain semantics must decide that issue.

## 8. Falsifiability Boundary

The minimal interpretive thesis is methodological and cannot be falsified as a
universal slogan. A domain-specific NZUI hypothesis must be rejectable.

Examples of failure conditions include:

- the proposed reservoir cannot account for the measured discrepancy;
- the transition law contradicts repeated observations;
- the model adds no explanatory or predictive value over a simpler model;
- conservation is asserted only by assigning all discrepancies to an
  unobservable infinity;
- the selected quantity is not conserved in the relevant theory or experiment.

An unresolved discrepancy should remain an Open Hold. It is not positive
evidence for hidden persistence.

## 9. Research Agenda

1. Select one narrow physical or informational domain.
2. Define its quantities and observational boundary using established notation.
3. Build a finite NZCM representation of the domain.
4. Identify where the representation differs from existing accounting.
5. Derive at least one discriminating prediction or operational advantage.
6. Invite independent domain review before making cosmological claims.
7. Publish negative results and abandoned mappings.

## 10. Interpretive Value

Even without becoming a physical theory, NZUI can serve as an epistemic warning:

> Do not convert a limit of observation into a claim of absolute absence.

Its value is strongest when it keeps inquiry open without filling every unknown
with an unfalsifiable reservoir.
